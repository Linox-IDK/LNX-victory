# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

LNX-victory is an open-source, community-driven project built by developers and contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and technical interests.

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone participating in the project.

Our goal is to build software together, exchange knowledge, solve difficult technical problems, and help the project evolve.

Everyone participating in the LNX-victory community is expected to help maintain this environment.

## Expected Behaviour

We expect all community members to:

- Be respectful, welcoming, and considerate.
- Treat everyone fairly, regardless of experience or skill level.
- Provide constructive technical feedback.
- Accept different opinions and approaches.
- Give credit to original authors and contributors.
- Be patient when helping newcomers.
- Focus discussions on ideas, implementations, and evidence.
- Communicate professionally during disagreements.

You do not need to be an expert to contribute. Questions, suggestions, and learning are welcome.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Threats, intimidation, or deliberate hostility.
- Insulting or humiliating other contributors.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting development or community discussions.
- Plagiarism or claiming another person's work as your own.
- Repeatedly provoking arguments or creating a hostile environment.
- Sharing malicious files or intentionally harmful code.

Disagreements about technical decisions are normal. Personal attacks are not.

## Technical Discussions

LNX-victory encourages open technical discussions.

Contributors are free to disagree about architecture, implementation choices, optimization strategies, and project direction.

However:

- Criticism should target the implementation, not the developer.
- Technical claims should be supported by evidence whenever possible.
- Alternative solutions should be discussed constructively.
- Contributors should respect maintainers' final decisions regarding project integration.

Being experienced does not give anyone permission to disrespect others.

## Collaboration & Attribution

Contributions should be properly credited.

- Do not claim work you did not create.
- Preserve relevant copyright and license notices.
- Acknowledge other contributors when using or adapting their work.
- Respect third-party licenses.
- Be transparent about substantial AI-assisted contributions when requested by maintainers or required by the relevant contribution rules.

Everyone's work deserves appropriate recognition.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances of a violation, maintainers may:

1. Issue a warning.
2. Remove inappropriate content.
3. Temporarily restrict participation.
4. Close discussions or contributions.
5. Remove a contributor from the project or community.

Serious or repeated violations may result in immediate removal.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the situation.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the project maintainers through the repository's available contact channels.

Reports should include relevant context and evidence when possible.

Reports will be handled as respectfully and confidentially as reasonably possible.

Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all LNX-victory project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories.
- Issues and pull requests.
- Discussions.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Other spaces officially representing LNX-victory.

## Final Statement

LNX-victory is built through collaboration.

We want contributors to feel comfortable sharing ideas, asking questions, experimenting, making mistakes, and learning from one another.

A healthy community is just as important as good code.

**Be respectful. Build together. Give credit. Keep improving.**
